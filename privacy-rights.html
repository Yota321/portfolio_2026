Overview

One Page,Everything.

Privacy, cookies, ownership, third-party credits, AI usage, terms of use. I put all of it on one page instead of five separate documents nobody was going to read anyway.

Quick version: I track basic analytics so I know the site works, the case studies and writing here are mine unless stated otherwise, a couple of photos are borrowed and credited, and none of it is here to train a model. The full version is below.

01. Privacy

What ICollect.

There's no login, no form, no checkout on this site, so there's nothing personal to hand over just to look around. What it does pick up is the usual low-key stuff most websites pick up: how many people visit, which pages hold their attention, what device and browser they're on, and roughly where they're browsing from. None of it is tied to a name or an identity.

That data comes from Google Analytics (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity, which also records anonymized session behaviour like scroll depth and click patterns so I can see which parts of the portfolio actually land. Neither tool is used to build a profile on you, sell data, or target ads.

Collected
Analytics & Behaviour
Page views, session length, scroll depth, general device and location data via GA4 and Clarity.
Collected
Basic Technical Data
Browser type, screen size, and referrer. Standard information any web server logs automatically.
Not Collected
Accounts & Passwords
There's no sign-up on this site. Nothing to create, nothing to lose, nothing to leak.
Not Collected
Payment Information
This portfolio doesn't sell anything, so no card, billing, or financial data ever touches it.
02. Cookies

Small Files,Clear Job.

Cookies and similar local-storage tech are used here for one reason, to understand how the site performs and how people move through it. That covers analytics through GA4 and Clarity, remembering small preferences like your chosen colour theme, and generally making repeat visits feel smoother.

None of it goes toward ad targeting, and nothing gets sold to a third party. Block or clear cookies in your browser whenever you want. The site still works fine, you just lose the saved theme preference and I lose a data point.
